# Placard

**Your places. On your cards. On your server.**

Placard is an **open-source, self-hosted place library** for the places you want to remember, revisit, and share decisions about. Bring your saved places together, enrich them with your own details, and make them useful beyond a pin on a map.

[**Website**](https://placard.fyi) · [**Documentation**](https://placard.fyi/docs) · [**Get started**](https://github.com/placard-app/core/blob/main/docs/install.md)

## Why Placard?

- **Your places, your data.** Run Placard on your own server with Docker Compose.
- **A library worth keeping.** Import saved places, add notes and photos, and find what matters with search and filters.
- **More than bookmarks.** Extend your library with optional modules for menus, outings, decisions, and more.
- **AI-friendly.** Connect compatible assistants through MCP to work with your place library.
- **Built for everyday use.** A web app with installable PWA support, designed around your own collection rather than public map discovery.
